HolyVerse Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

HolyVerse ("the App", "we") is developed and operated by Jeancarlo Ricardo, an independent developer based in Ontario, Canada. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect:

  • Account information: your name and email address, if you create an account with email/password or sign in with Google.
  • Content you save: favorite verses, highlighted verses, and (in the future) personal notes you add to a verse.
  • Usage preferences: language, theme (light/dark), and mood selections used to recommend relevant verses.
  • Bible Bot messages: questions you send to our AI-powered Bible assistant are sent to generate a response, but are not stored tied to your identity beyond what's needed to process that individual conversation.
  • Analytics data: we use Google Analytics (through Firebase) to understand how the App is used in aggregate — which screens are visited, how many people use each feature. This includes anonymous device identifiers, not your name or email.

2. How We Store Your Information

If you use the App without signing in ("guest mode"), your favorites and highlights are stored only in your own browser or device's local storage — they never reach our servers. If you create an account, that information (along with anything saved as a guest) syncs to a secure database (Firebase Firestore, by Google), protected by access rules that ensure only you can read or modify your own data.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

To operate the App, we rely on the following providers, each with their own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google): account authentication and user data storage.
  • Anthropic: processes Bible Bot conversations to generate AI-powered responses.
  • Railway: hosts our own server that provides Bible text, Strong's data, character profiles, and the daily devotional.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the App's website.
  • Google Analytics: collects aggregated, anonymous usage statistics to help us improve the App.

4. Your Rights and Choices

  • You can remove a favorite or highlight at any time from your profile.
  • You can sign out at any time from your profile.
  • You can request full deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing jeanca979@gmail.com. We will process that request manually and confirm once it's complete.

5. Children

HolyVerse is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including database-level security rules that restrict each user's data access to that user alone. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will indicate this by updating the date at the top of this document.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at jeanca979@gmail.com.
